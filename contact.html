Questions

Things people ask before booking

For an airport run or a local trip, a day is usually enough. For the Chardham season between May and June, or for a Tempo Traveller, book a few weeks ahead — vehicles genuinely run out.

Cash, UPI or bank transfer. For multi-day packages we usually take a small advance to hold the vehicle and the rest at the end of the trip. We will confirm the exact terms when we quote.

Tell us as early as you can and we will do our best. For long packages where we have turned away other work, some advance may be retained — we will always say so upfront rather than surprise you.

Night driving and halts are built into the quote we give you. You will not be asked for extra at the end of a late run.

The driver arranges his own stay and meals — his allowance is already in your quote. You are not expected to book or pay for his room.

We do not sell hotel bookings, but we know the towns on these routes well and will happily point you to places that have treated our passengers properly.
