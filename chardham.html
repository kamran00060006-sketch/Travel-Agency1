The four dhams

A journey people plan once in a lifetime.

We are a Rishikesh family running cars on these roads for years. We know where the traffic holds up, where elders need a rest stop, and when to start so you reach darshan in time.

Gangotri

Where the Ganga is worshipped at her source. Road goes right up to the temple.

Kedarnath

Trek or helicopter from Sonprayag. We park and wait for as long as you need.

Badrinath

The most accessible of the four, with Mana village a short drive further.

Before you go

A few things worth knowing.

Small preparations make a long difference on these roads. Ask us anything on the phone — we would rather explain than have you arrive unprepared.

Best season

The shrines open around late April or early May and close near Diwali. May–June and September–October are the steadiest. The monsoon between July and August brings landslides and road closures.

Registration

Uttarakhand requires yatra registration for the Chardham. Carry your photo ID and keep a printed copy. We will tell you what is needed for your dates.

Health and altitude

Kedarnath sits above 11,000 ft. Carry prescribed medicines, warm layers and rain cover, and keep the first day light. Tell us in advance if anyone in the group has a heart or breathing condition.
