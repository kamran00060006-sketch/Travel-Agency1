The one people ask for

Toyota Innova Crysta

The Crysta is the car we recommend for almost every hill trip, and the one families ask for by name. It holds the road on long descents, the suspension takes rough patches without punishing anyone inside, and there is genuine room for seven people and their bags.

For elderly passengers on the Chardham route it makes a real difference — a higher seat to get into, space to stretch on long days, and air conditioning that works at altitude.

How we price

No meter games

We quote a figure before the trip and that is the figure you pay. It covers the car, the driver, fuel, tolls, parking and the driver's night allowance where the trip needs one.

If the plan changes on the road — an extra stop, an extra day — we tell you what it adds before it is added, not after.

Included in every quote

  • Vehicle and driver for the agreed duration
  • Fuel, tolls and parking
  • State permits where required
  • Driver night allowance on multi-day trips

Quoted separately:

  • Hotels and meals
  • Helicopter, pony or palki on the yatra
  • Anything personal in nature
