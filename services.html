Taxi, tour and travel services

From a short airport run to a two-week tour of the hills — one number covers all of it.

Across the state

Rishikesh to anywhere in Uttarakhand.

Char Dham circuit, the hill stations, the lakes and the parks. Drivers who have run these routes in every season.

Haridwar & Dehradun

Short, frequent runs. Har Ki Pauri for the evening aarti, or Dehradun for the railway station and the airport.

Mussoorie & Dhanaulti

Queen of the hills, comfortable as a day trip or an overnight. Kempty Falls, Mall Road and the Dhanaulti deodars.

Chopta & Tungnath

The mini Switzerland of Uttarakhand, and the highest Shiva temple in the world. Best done over two days.

Auli & Joshimath

Meadows, the cable car and Nanda Devi on a clear morning. Snow between January and March.

Nainital & Kausani

The Kumaon side — lakes, ridges and long Himalayan views. Usually a three to four day loop.

Jim Corbett

Drop and pick up for safari stays, or a full package with the vehicle waiting through your visit.

Airport transfer

Picked up on time, every time

Send your flight number when you book. If the flight is delayed the driver waits — we track it rather than leaving.

Jolly Grant Airport (DED)

About 20 km from Rishikesh, usually 35–45 minutes. The most common transfer we run.

  • Meet and greet at arrivals with a name board on request
  • Flight tracked — no charge for reasonable delay
  • Innova Crysta for families with luggage

Delhi Airport (DEL)

Around 240 km, roughly 6 hours depending on traffic on the Delhi–Dehradun corridor.

  • Overnight and very early morning departures available
  • One-way or round trip
  • Comfortable for long transfers with elders or small children
Outstation

One-way and round trip, honestly priced.

If you are travelling one way, ask for the one-way fare. Plenty of operators quote a round trip regardless — we do not.

One-way drop

You pay for the distance you actually travel rather than the car's return leg. Best when you are flying out from the other end or continuing your journey.

  • Delhi, Chandigarh, Shimla, Jaipur, Agra and beyond
  • No hidden return charge

Round trip

The vehicle stays with you for the whole journey, stopping wherever you like. Simplest for multi-day tours and pilgrimages.

  • Car at your disposal for the full duration
  • Driver allowance and night halt included in the quote
All India

Beyond the state lines

We take bookings anywhere in India. Long-distance runs are planned with permits arranged in advance, night halts built in, and a driver who is rested rather than pushed.

Tell us the destination and dates — if it is a route we should not take on, we will say so and help you find someone who can.

Local sightseeing

Rishikesh, properly seen

A half or full day around town with the car waiting at each stop.

Laxman Jhula & Ram Jhula

The two iron footbridges the town is known for, with the ghats and markets on either bank.

Triveni Ghat Ganga Aarti

The evening lamp ceremony on the river. Go early for a place near the front.

Beatles Ashram

The old Maharishi Mahesh Yogi ashram, now covered in murals and quiet forest paths.

Neelkanth Mahadev

A temple up in the hills about 32 km away, through forest the whole way.

Kunjapuri sunrise

An early start for Himalayan peaks at first light. We leave around 4 am.

Vashishta Gufa

A cave on the Ganga about 25 km up the Devprayag road. Still and rarely crowded.
